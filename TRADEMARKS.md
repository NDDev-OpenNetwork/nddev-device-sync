# NDDev OpenNetwork branding

The software is released under GNU AGPL-3.0-only. The names `NDDev`,
`NDDev OpenNetwork`, `nddev.ai`, associated logos and product marks remain
NDDev branding.

Self-hosted instances must retain the NDDev OpenNetwork name, attribution and
link to https://nddev.ai in the standard UI, about surface, source offer and
documentation. Local operators may add their own instance name and contact
information. Removing or replacing the required NDDev attribution is outside
the standard self-hosting configuration and requires a separate trademark
policy decision.
